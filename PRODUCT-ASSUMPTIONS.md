NEARTASK-Product-Assumptions
1.What product are you planning to build?
We are planning to build an app where you can hire local people to do tasks or you do others task and earn cash.No middle man or any other hidden fees
2.What problem does it solve?
It solves the problem of having to spend time working on something which you would rather pay for someone else to do or if you needed some expertise in some domains without the need to contact large corporations and such
3.Who are the target users?
College Students,People who can't afford to have some free time.
4.What is the main use case?
The main use case is it provides quick access to contact people to get work done ,and having the power to negotiate the price too
5.What are the core features?
The core features are browser searches,transparent prices,chat,profile,search filter
6.What assumptions are you making about the users?
Assumptions like: do students struggle to find tutors,designers,technicians and do the people having some basic skills have the place to  provide their services. 
7.What will be included in your MVP?
Browse,View providers profile,check booking,,allow providers to reject or accept tasks,submit booking request
8.What will NOT be included in the MVP?
Online Transaction,GPS, Chatting

Our idea is inspired by Ripplet,A local service providing/taking marketplace.NearTask is inspired by this and it provides students a place to find tutors ,laptop technicians or provide services themselves.
